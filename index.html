For house hunting on foot

You photographed the gate. Which house was it?

RentPin saves each to-let gate on a private map with one tap, and reads the phone number off the sign. Later, your own photos show you where every house is.

Recorded from the real app. The demo photos are AI-generated and the phone numbers are fictional.

At the gate

One tap, and it's saved.

There's no form and no Save button. The photo saves when you take it, with the location, and the number on the sign is read for you. Keep shooting inside and those photos join the same house.

That evening

Your gates are the pins.

You remember a gate better than a phone number. Every pin is your own photo, with a coloured ring for its status: to call, called, visited, liked or rejected.

When you're deciding

Call, compare, go back.

Call with one tap and get directions in Google Maps. Add rent, service charge and bedrooms, and tick features like line gas, lift or parking. Filter by monthly cost or must-have features to see only what's still worth considering.

Deciding together?

Share houses with your family.

Pick one house or several, then send them to a group chat. Each one becomes a picture card with its photos, number, rent, features and map link. Your notes and status stay private unless you include them.

Already have photos?

Bring in your camera roll.

Pick your old gate photos. RentPin places them using the location saved in each photo and groups shots of the same house. Any photo with no location waits for you to drop it on the map.

Your house hunt stays yours.

  • No account needed. Your own map never asks you to sign up.
  • No tracking. No analytics and no ads.
  • Stored on your phone. Houses and photos stay in the app unless you choose to share them. Backups go only where you save them.

The map comes from OpenFreeMap on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone, so they receive the map requests, as with any map app. Directions open Google Maps only when you tap them. Calls go through your phone as usual. The optional community map is in early testing around Dhaka on Android; nothing is shared unless you choose to share it. Read the privacy policy.
