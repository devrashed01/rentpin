Community rules and terms

Last updated 8 November 2026

RentPin is made by Eahea Ratan. By using RentPin you agree to these terms. Sharing is optional, and these rules apply when you do.

Before you pay anyone

Never pay an advance before you've seen the house in person and met the owner. Listings are shared by other renters and aren't checked by RentPin. RentPin is not a broker or property agent, and takes no part in any rental.

What you can share

  • Real to-let signs and houses that are really for rent.
  • Photos you took yourself, with no people or car number plates visible.
  • The phone number shown on that to-let sign, or your own number if it's your house.
  • An accurate pin, rent and details, as far as you know them.

What you can't do

  • Post fake listings, or ask for money through a listing.
  • Post someone's private number that wasn't put up for renting.
  • Post anything abusive, sexual, hateful or illegal.
  • Use a revealed number for anything except asking about that house. That means no marketing, spam, harassment or resale.
  • Collect listings or numbers in bulk, or try to get around the limits or security.
  • Use more than one account to avoid a limit or a ban.

How the community stays honest

  • A new account's first posts wait for review before they appear.
  • There are daily limits on posting and on revealing numbers. They can change.
  • Three reports from different people hide a listing until it's reviewed.
  • Listings with no recent confirmation fade, then leave the map.

We may hide or remove any listing, and suspend or ban any account, if we believe these rules were broken or someone could be harmed.

Your content

What you share stays yours. By publishing it, you allow RentPin to store it and show it to other people in the app for as long as it's published. You're responsible for what you post. You can unpublish it at any time.

If your number is listed

Anyone can ask for their number to be removed, with no account, on the removal page.

No guarantees

RentPin is provided free and as it is. Information on the community map can be wrong or out of date, and the app can have faults or stop being available. Back up anything you can't afford to lose. To the extent the law allows, RentPin and its maker aren't liable for losses from using the app, from listings, or from dealings with other people.
