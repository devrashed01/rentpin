Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

RentPin is made by Eahea Ratan. Questions or requests go to devrashed01@gmail.com.

The short version: your own house hunt stays on your phone. Something leaves your phone only when you choose to share it on the community map.

Using RentPin privately

You don't need an account. Your houses, photos, notes and statuses are stored in the app on your phone. Phone numbers on signs are read on your phone. We can't see any of this.

Backups go only where you save them. Picture cards go only to the people you send them to.

A few things go to other companies because of how phones and maps work:

  • Map images come from OpenFreeMap on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. They receive your internet address and the area you're viewing.
  • Directions open Google Maps, and only when you tap them.
  • Calls go through your phone as usual.

The community map (optional)

The community map is being tested in Dhaka and isn't in the current download yet. This section explains how it works.

Browsing needs no account. The app asks our server for listings in the area on your screen.

Signing in uses Google. Google gives us your email address, name and profile picture. We use them to run your account and stop abuse. They are never shown to other people.

When you share a house, you see exactly what becomes public before you publish:

  • The photos you choose, resized and re-saved so the location and camera details are removed.
  • The pin on the map, which is the exact place you marked.
  • Rent, service charge, advance, bedrooms, floor, features and the date it was seen.

Listings say "Shared by a renter". Your notes, your status, your other photos and your name are never shared.

The phone number on the sign is stored separately and isn't public. It's shown only to someone who signs in and taps to reveal it, and each person has a daily limit. We record who revealed which number and when, to prevent harvesting.

We also store when you accepted the rules, your "still available" and "rented out" answers, your reports, and the people you've blocked.

Who can see what: only the RentPin administrator can see who posted a listing, along with numbers and reports, in order to review them.

How long we keep it

  • A listing leaves the map after 30 days without anyone confirming it.
  • When you unpublish a listing, its photos and phone number are deleted. A basic record stays so that daily limits still work.
  • When you delete your account in the app, your account, listings, numbers, photos, answers, reports and reveal history are deleted.
  • Service providers' logs and backups can hold copies for a short time afterwards.

If your number is on the map

Your number was on a public sign, and someone shared that sign. You can ask for it to be removed without an account, using the removal page. Any listing with the number is hidden straight away, then reviewed.

We store the request as a one-way code made from the number, not as the number itself, along with any note you add. If the removal is approved, the number is deleted and the code stays so it can't be posted again.

Who helps run RentPin

  • Supabase stores community accounts, listings and photos. Its servers may be outside Bangladesh.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the Android download, and may log visitors' internet addresses.

RentPin has no ads or analytics, and we don't sell your information.

Your choices

You can unpublish a listing or delete your account in the app at any time. To ask for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, email devrashed01@gmail.com.

RentPin's community features are for people aged 18 or over.

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. If a change affects what you've already shared, we'll tell you in the app.
